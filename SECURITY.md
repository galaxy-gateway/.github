# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately. Do not open a public issue, discussion, or pull request.

Use GitHub private vulnerability reporting: go to the **Security** tab of the affected repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**. If the repository does not offer it, open the form at https://github.com/galaxy-gateway/.github/security/advisories/new or ask for a private channel in a minimal public issue that contains no details.

Include the affected repo and version, steps to reproduce, and the impact you see.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge reports within 7 days.
- We aim to ship a fix or mitigation within 90 days.
- We follow 90-day coordinated disclosure: details become public after a fix ships, or after 90 days, whichever comes first. We will agree on an earlier or later date with you if the situation calls for it.
- We credit reporters in the advisory unless you prefer not to be named.

## Safe harbor

If you make a good-faith effort to follow this policy, we will not pursue or support legal action against you for your research. Good faith means: test only against your own accounts and data, do not degrade services or access other people's data, stop and report as soon as you confirm an issue, and give us a reasonable time to fix it before disclosure.

## Scope and rewards

This applies to every public repository in the galaxy-gateway organization. We do not run a bounty program and cannot offer payment, but we are grateful for the report.
